# Arcade audio and HUD assets

The optional shared music uses the two OGG Vorbis files already extracted from the user's Pirate Bomb Brawl HTML: `public/pirate-brawl/audio/menu.ogg` (Clubhouse) and `battle.ogg` (Arena). Their original bytes, sizes and SHA-256 hashes remain in `public/pirate-brawl/manifest.json`; provenance is in [pirate-brawl.md](pirate-brawl.md). No new recording was downloaded or re-encoded.

The three later uploads—Towball's Crossing, Kart Racer by juanjo_sound, and Peritune Sparkling Sea—each exceed the execution environment's 32 MiB attachment transfer limit. Their contents and individual licenses could not be inspected; no recording from those ZIPs is included. Metadata was checked at the creators' pages: https://towball.itch.io/towballs-crossing, https://juanjosound.itch.io/kart-racer-free-music-pack and https://booth.pm/ja/items/4022495. The local music picker accepts an individual audio file up to 25 MiB, creates a browser-only object URL and revokes it on replacement or disposal. It does not upload the recording or share it with other players.

Sound effects are short synthesized tones/noise from Web Audio. Music and effects default off, stream only after opt-in, stop while the document is hidden, and clean up when leaving the game view. Music uses one reusable media element; effects use one lazy context, a reused noise buffer and at most eight voices. The drawing pencil cue is local; other cues use small public game counters and never inspect hidden cards, words or terrain.

The Pirate HUD uses the upload's own character and heart PNGs. `public/fonts/press-start-2p.woff` is a WOFF conversion of the official Google Fonts `ofl/pressstart2p/PressStart2P-Regular.ttf`, downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/pressstart2p/PressStart2P-Regular.ttf. The SIL Open Font License is retained in [licenses/press-start-2p-OFL.txt](licenses/press-start-2p-OFL.txt). It is used only by the Pirate HUD and is not preloaded on other pages. The ring crosshair is drawn in code, shared by Pirate and Tank Battle, and scales with the display rather than camera zoom.
